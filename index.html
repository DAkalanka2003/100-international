The Studio

A production house
that never runs out
of takes.

100 International Universe is a film and advertising studio built around generative AI pipelines and a dedicated team of directors, editors and sound designers who steer them. No sets to strike, no weather to wait out — just faster iteration toward the shot that works.

The studio was founded on a simple bet: that the bottleneck in film production has always been logistics, not imagination. Remove the logistics, and a small, obsessive team can output at a scale that used to need a backlot.

The Pipeline

From brief to
final master.

Five stages take a project from a one-page idea to a mastered, market-ready film in days, not months.

  1. 01

    Concept & script

    Directors and writers turn a brief into a cinematic script, shot list, lighting palette and visual reference board.

  2. 02

    AI multi-take generation

    Custom trained pipelines generate character performance, complex camera passes, and environments across hundreds of takes in parallel.

  3. 03

    Human direction & assembly

    Senior editors and colourists select, assemble, and grade — every single frame approved by a human creative director.

  4. 04

    Sound, score & Foley

    Original score, sound design, dialogue enhancement and Dolby/5.1 mix layered against picture lock for cinema impact.

  5. 05

    Mastering & global delivery

    Mastered for broadcast, social and cinema — in every aspect ratio, color space (DCI-P3/Rec.709) and regional language needed.
